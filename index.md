# Folio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Folio is a receipt capture tool for Hargrove Engineers & Constructors employees, on iPhone and Android.

## What Folio stores
Receipt images and the details read from them (vendor, date, amount, category, charge number, attendees, trip), and any mileage entries you add, are stored on your device. Your phone's own backup may include them, depending on your settings.

## How receipts are read
Text recognition and extraction run on your device, using the phone's built-in text recognition and, where available, its on-device language model. Receipt content is not sent to Folio's developer or to any third-party service by Folio itself.

## Web links
If you share a web link to Folio, Folio loads that page from the site that hosts it, the same way your browser would, without your browser's sign-ins, and saves it as a receipt on your device. Links to private network addresses are refused.

## What leaves your device
Only what you choose to send. When you export a trip, Folio builds the receipt files and a summary spreadsheet and hands them to the app you pick, addressed to the recipients you choose; it also copies those recipients' addresses so you can paste them, and the copy stays on this phone and clears itself after ten minutes. If you use Send Feedback, the message you write is sent by email. If you choose to paste a receipt's scanner text into it, as the feedback email suggests when reporting a misread receipt, that text is sent with your message.

## What Folio does not do
No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Folio itself does not collect data about you.

## Android: Google ML Kit
On Android, Folio reads receipt text with Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device. ML Kit sends Google limited technical information about its own operation: device information, the app's name and version, an installation identifier, and performance and error data. It does not send receipt images, receipt text or amounts. See Google's ML Kit terms for details.

## Crash reports
If you allow it in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports with the developer.

## Removing your data
Deleting a receipt removes it from the device. Deleting Folio removes all of its data.
